Global Connectivity Solutions

Comprehensive internet infrastructure services seamlessly bridging national backbones to intercontinental submarine cables, delivering the world to your business with ultra-low latency.

Vietnam National Backbone

Ultra-broadband national backbone network connecting major data centers across Vietnam (Hanoi, Da Nang, HCMC) directly, ensuring the lowest local latency and a comprehensive Ring Topology redundancy system.

Cross-Border Terrestrial Transit

Unlocking high-speed cross-border terrestrial connectivity from Vietnam through Laos, Cambodia, and Thailand, reaching international transit hubs in Malaysia and Singapore independently of submarine cables.

Submarine Cable Connectivity

Massive bandwidth transmission solutions via high-speed international submarine fiber optic systems. Direct connections from landing stations (Da Nang, Quy Nhon, Vung Tau) straight to Hong Kong and Singapore.

Global IP Transit & Peering

Global IP routing solutions providing direct access to the Internet routing table (BGP) and direct peering with major content providers (Tier-1 ISPs, Cloud Providers) to optimize your network path.
